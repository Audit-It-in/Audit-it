# Product Overview

## Audit-it Platform

A NoBroker-style Chartered Accountant (CA) listing platform that connects customers with qualified CAs for tax, audit, and compliance services.

## Key Features

- **CA Discovery**: Search and filter CAs by location, specialization, and services
- **Profile Management**: Comprehensive CA profiles with verification, experience, and education
- **Contact System**: Direct communication between customers and CAs
- **Mobile-First**: Optimized for mobile devices with responsive design

## User Roles

- **Accountants (CAs)**: Create profiles, manage contact requests, showcase services
- **Customers**: Search CAs, send contact requests, compare services
- **Admins**: Platform management and content moderation

## Business Model

Platform facilitating CA-customer connections with focus on:

- Professional verification and credibility
- Location-based service discovery
- Streamlined communication workflow
- Quality service provider network

## SEO Strategy

- **Display**: Uses "CA" and "Chartered Accountant" terminology for SEO optimization
- **Internal**: Uses "accountant" terminology in code for consistency and clarity
- **Target**: "CA near me" and related search queries

## Success Metrics

- 50+ verified CAs in first quarter
- 20+ contact requests per week
- 15%+ contact-to-engagement conversion rate
- 4.5+ average CA rating

## Development Standards

### Quality Requirements

- **Component Size**: Maximum 200 lines per component
- **Test Coverage**: 90%+ across critical components
- **Performance**: Lighthouse scores 90+ on mobile
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliance required
- **Type Safety**: Strict TypeScript with enum-driven development

### Terminology Standards

- **User-Facing**: "CA", "Chartered Accountant" (for SEO optimization)
- **Internal Code**: "accountant" (for consistency and clarity)
- **Database**: `role: 'accountant'` (technical implementation)
- **URLs**: `/accountants` (clean, descriptive routing)
